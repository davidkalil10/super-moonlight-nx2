# Moonlight NX2 features

## Connection and pairing
- Talks to Sunshine directly; nothing else runs on the PC
- Pairing with a 4-digit PIN entered in Sunshine's web UI
- A separate console identity per PC, so several PCs pair side by side
- Forget a PC, then pair it again with a fresh identity
- Find PCs on the local network, or Add a PC by address
- Wake PC (Wake-on-LAN)

## Apps
- App list with cover art
- Launch, resume and switch apps
- Disconnect, or quit the app on the PC
- Quit host app on return

## Video
- AV1 on the console's hardware decoder, with H.264 as the fallback
- 120 fps by default on a 120 Hz display, handheld included; docked at 1440p or 1080p
- Automatic, 720p or 1080p; 1440p and 4K when docked (4K at 60 fps)
- The stream switches resolution when you dock or undock
- Bitrate from a network test, or 5–150 Mbps
- Optimise game settings: the PC display follows the stream's size and rate
- Stream info badge and frame counter, with a latency breakdown in Full stats
- A warning when the network can't keep up

## Audio
- Stereo audio on the console's hardware decoder
- Play audio on the PC instead

## Input
- Up to 4 controllers in the Switch's player order, handheld included
- Motion: Sunshine emulates a DualShock 4 or DualSense, so games get gyro
- Rumble, with adjustable strength
- Touchscreen, or touch as mouse
- Virtual gamepad: on-screen controls in handheld mode with analog sticks, sent as an extra Xbox controller
- Tap a button hint on screen to press it
- Button layout: Xbox (by position) or Nintendo (by label)
- USB keyboard and mouse
- Joy-Con mouse: a Joy-Con laid on a table moves the PC mouse, with adjustable sensitivity

## Stream controls
- Hold Plus+Minus to open the stream menu, from any player; the PC never sees the chord
- L+R+Minus+Plus stops the stream at once
- On-screen keyboard that types on the PC
- Power: keep the console's full clocks while streaming, or save battery

## App
- Settings saved per app
- System, light or dark theme
- A runtime log on the SD card for bug reports
